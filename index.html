How to play
  • Roll the dice and travel around Tamil Nadu.
  • Land on an unowned place? Its deed card appears — buy it or skip.
  • Land on someone's property? You pay rent. A full colour group doubles the rent.
  • Railway junctions earn ₹600 × junctions owned. TNEB & Metro Water charge dice × ₹150 (× ₹300 with both).
  • Sandhippu (சந்திப்பு) tiles draw a surprise card.
  • In the lockup? Pay ₹500 or roll doubles (3 tries).
  • Bankrupt players are out; their places return to the bank.
  • Win by outlasting everyone — or be the richest (cash + property) when a timed game ends.
